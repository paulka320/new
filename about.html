About St. Anthony Doctors' Avenue

St. Anthony Doctors’ Avenue is a modern, patient-centered healthcare institution established in Soroti City, Eastern Uganda, dedicated to delivering safe, affordable and high quality healthcare.

We bring together clinical expertise, compassionate care, modern technology, innovation and evidence based practice to provide an exceptional patient experience and better health outcomes.

Our ambition is to become a trusted Centre of healthcare excellence in Eastern Uganda and beyond, while contributing to stronger communities through accessible care, professional development, research and strategic partnerships.

“Where Expertise Meets Compassion For Healthier Lives”

Dr. Oseko Anthony

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
MBChB, DHSM/L, DCM

Dr. Oseko Anthony is a healthcare professional and health service manager with over eight years of progressive experience across public, private not for profit and private for profit health care settings. He has served in health facility leadership and management roles, gaining practical expertise in healthcare operations, service delivery, quality improvement and institutional development.

With academic training in Health Services Management, Dr Oseko combines Clinical and managerial expertise to advance patient centered care, operational excellence, innovation, integrity and sustainable healthcare development.

As CEO of St. Anthony Doctors’ Avenue, his vision is to build a trusted, modern and accessible healthcare institution that delivers excellence in care and better health outcomes for communities in Eastern Uganda and beyond.

Ms. Sarah Aluko

Chief Operations Manager
MBA, PG-M/E, BQE

Ms. Sarah Aluko is a dedicated results oriented professional with over 6 years of serving as the Chief Operations Manager at St. Anthony Doctors’ Avenue. She brings strong expertise in programme co-ordination, healthcare operations, monitoring and evaluation, performance improvement and stakeholder engagement.

With postgraduate academic training in Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E), Ms. Aluko combines practical experience with evidence driven management to strengthen service quality, accountability and organizational performance.

As our Chief Operations Manager, she is committed to building efficient, patient centered and accountable systems that translate the organization’s vision, mission and core values into exceptional healthcare delivery.
