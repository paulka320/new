Community Wellness

Daily Health Tips

Practical, doctor-approved medical advice to protect yourself, your family, and the Soroti community every day.

Water & Hygiene

Drink boiled or treated water daily

Ensure all drinking and cooking water is thoroughly boiled for at least 3 minutes or treated with water guard to prevent waterborne infections like typhoid and cholera.

Store clean drinking water in covered, hygienic containers.
Infection Control

Sleep under treated mosquito nets

Tuck long-lasting insecticide-treated nets securely under your mattress every night. Clear empty tins, plastic containers, and stagnant puddles near your compound.

Protect children under 5 and expectant mothers consistently.
Preventive Health

Check your blood pressure regularly

High blood pressure rarely has early symptoms. Adults 25 and older should get a baseline reading every 6 months to detect and manage cardiovascular risk factors early.

Walk-in blood pressure checks available at our outpatient clinic.
Maternal & Child

Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months

Breast milk provides complete nutrition, sterile hydration, and vital immune defenses for newborns during their first 6 months. Begin weaning with iron-rich local foods.

Attend all scheduled antenatal & infant immunisation rounds.
Pediatric Care

Never ignore persistent fevers in children

A high fever accompanied by lethargy, poor feeding, vomiting, or rapid breathing requires urgent clinical assessment rather than unguided home self-medication.

Our pediatric emergency team is open 24 hours every day.
Daily Hygiene

Wash hands with clean soap & running water

Washing hands thoroughly for at least 20 seconds before food prep, before eating, and after using toilets cuts diarrheal infections and respiratory illness transmission by over 40%.

Lather soap thoroughly between fingers and across nail beds.
